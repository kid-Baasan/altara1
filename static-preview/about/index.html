About

Our Story

Founded On
A Simple Belief

Altara began in Thimphu with a small team who had spent years guiding travelers across Bhutan for other operators — and grew tired of watching remarkable journeys get flattened into fixed-departure packages.

We started Altara to do the opposite: build each itinerary from scratch, around one traveler or family at a time, with the same guides, drivers, and homes we'd trust for our own. Fifteen years later, that's still the entire business — no templates, no fixed groups, no script.

What began as a handful of trips a year has grown into a full journey house, but the promise hasn't changed: every trip is still built by hand, for the person taking it.

Our Philosophy

Travel As
Transformation

Bhutan is not a destination to be checked off — it is a country that asks you to slow down. Altara was founded on the belief that true luxury is time: time to sit with a monastery bell echoing across a valley, time to walk beside a farmer without an agenda, time to simply be somewhere fully.

Every itinerary we build is private, unhurried, and shaped around a single traveler or family. We work only with a small circle of guides, drivers, and homes we trust completely — so the plan disappears, and the place gets to do the rest.
